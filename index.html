About

I’m Daria, and I make drawings, paintings and animation. I like to understand how things are built as well as how they look, so my work moves between loose watercolour and cartoon, careful studies of light and shadow, and precise technical drawing.

This page shows one piece from each of the eight styles I work in. The folders on Google Drive hold more.
